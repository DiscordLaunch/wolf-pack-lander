Why this room

Every paid community has known ways to die. The Wolf Pack is designed around the answers.

Big rooms drown in their own noise.

A curated founding cohort and a culture that values substance over volume. The room is built for serious peers, not maximum headcount.

Questions

What you'd want to know.

What is The Wolf Pack?
A curated community where Scott opens up the team, research and network behind his work, and members participate through questions, discussion and relationships with each other.
What does access include?
The member program defines the sessions, research, team participation and guest access currently included. The confirmed scope and cadence are published alongside the membership page so you know exactly what's in the room before you join.
Does "open" mean the research is public?
No. The community and its materials are provided through membership. Sharing and redistribution follow the membership terms. "Open sourcing" is the internal analogy for sharing resources with members, not a public license.
How do I join?
Use the official membership page on Whop. The link will be published with the confirmed program details before launch.
What is a Founding Member?
Founding Member is a permanent founding-status label, not a newly invented paid tier. Founding members receive a locked founding rate for the life of their membership and help establish the conversations and culture of the community.
